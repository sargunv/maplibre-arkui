Build [style expressions] with functions named after their operators, such as
<Api of="get" />, <Api of="match" />, <Api of="interpolate" />, and
<Api of="featureState" /> for `feature-state`. Symbols get words (`eq` for `==`,
`add` for `+`), and `case` is <Api of="cases" />, which takes a list of
<Api of="Condition" /> pairs. Use them in the `filter`, `paint`, and `layout` of a
layer in the [content](/maplibre-arkui/content/), and as the `filter` of a
[feature query](/maplibre-arkui/style/#query-features):

<Code code={region(examples, "expressions")} lang="ts" />

<Api of="fp" display="fp(value)" /> isn't an operator: it scales a `text-size`
by the font scale that the map sets, as ArkUI scales `fp` sizes.

<Api of="call" display="call(operator, ...args)" /> and
<Api of="Expression" display="new Expression(json)" /> cover the operators that
have no function. Expressions are untyped: MapLibre checks their types as the
map applies them, and an invalid expression in the content goes to the map's
`onError`.

[style expressions]: https://maplibre.org/maplibre-style-spec/expressions/
