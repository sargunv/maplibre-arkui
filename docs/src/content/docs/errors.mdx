- Methods throw an `Error` for an invalid argument, such as a zoom that isn't
  finite, before they start any work.

- Work that finishes later returns a promise. A camera move resolves with
  whether the camera got there; being stopped, such as by the user's input, is
  `false`, not a failure. A promise rejects with an `Error` when the work fails,
  such as a move or a query that the map rejects. The FFI map's own methods
  reject with FFI's errors, which extend `Error`.

- What goes wrong while the map runs, with no caller waiting for it, goes to
  the map's `onError` and to the log as a <Api of="MapError" />: a style that
  fails to load is a <Api of="StyleLoadError" />, and a source, layer, or image
  of the content that the map rejects is a <Api of="ContentError" /> with its
  ID. Other failures, such as a frame that fails to render or an invalid
  `viewportInsets` or `cameraBounds`, arrive as a plain `MapError`, and later
  releases may add kinds, so keep a fallback:

  <Code code={region(examples, "map-errors")} lang="ts" />

- An invalid map parameter goes to `onError` too, and the map uses its default
  or its last valid value in its place.

- `snapshot()` rejects when the style or a resource it needs fails to load.
