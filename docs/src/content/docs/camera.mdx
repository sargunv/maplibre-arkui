The camera decides which part of the map is on screen. A
<Api of="CameraPosition" /> has a center coordinate, a zoom level, a bearing,
and a pitch. At zoom 0 the whole world is 512 vp wide, and each zoom level
doubles the scale. Bearing is the compass direction at the top of the screen, in
degrees clockwise from north. Pitch tilts the map, in degrees away from looking
straight down.

A <Api of="MapController" /> reads and moves the camera, the way a `Scroller`
does for a list. Create one, pass it to the map, and keep it.

## Move the camera

<Api of="MapController.updateCamera" display="updateCamera()" /> changes the
fields that you pass and keeps the others. A <Api of="CameraTransitionMode" />
chooses how the camera gets there. `EASE` moves straight to the target, which
suits short moves such as zooming in:

<Code code={region(camera, "ease")} lang="ts" />

`FLY` zooms out, travels, and zooms back in, which suits moves between places
that are far apart. `JUMP`, the default, moves at once:

<Code code={region(camera, "fly")} lang="ts" />

## Fit an area on screen

To show a whole route or a set of search results, use
<Api of="MapController.fitCameraToBounds" display="fitCameraToBounds()" />.
`fitPadding` keeps space between the area and the edges of the map:

<Code code={region(camera, "fit")} lang="ts" />

The promise resolves `false` if something, such as the user's touch, stops the
camera before it arrives. To fit coordinates or a GeoJSON geometry instead, use
`fitCameraToCoordinates()` or `fitCameraToGeometry()`.

<Api of="MapController.cameraMoving" /> is true while the camera moves, and
<Api of="MapController.cameraMoveReason" /> says whether the user or the app
moved it. This panel hides while the user moves the map:

## Keep the camera clear of your UI

When your UI covers part of the map, such as a panel at the bottom, pass the
covered edges as the map's
<Api of="MaplibreMap.viewportInsets" display="viewportInsets" />. Camera moves
then center in the part of the map that the user can see, and the controls stay
clear of the panel:

<Code code={region(camera, "viewport-insets")} lang="ts" />
