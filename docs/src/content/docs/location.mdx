The map shows the location that the app passes as `userLocation`, a Location Kit
`geoLocationManager.Location`, as a dot in a circle of its accuracy, with an
arrow for its course while it moves.

<Api of="MapController.followUserLocation" display="followUserLocation()" />

moves the camera with each new location until the user pans, the app moves the
camera's center, or the app calls `stopFollowingUserLocation()`. The
controller's observable `followingUserLocation` shows whether it follows, so a
Locate me button can offer it again:

<Code code={region(examples, "location")} lang="ts" />

While the camera follows, the user can zoom, pitch, and rotate. Zoom and
rotation keep the location at the center, and two fingers zoom and rotate
without panning. The camera waits for the user's input to end, then catches up
with the location, after `onGestureEnd`. To keep the user from stopping it, turn
pan off with
[`interactions`](/maplibre-arkui/gestures/#choose-the-cameras-movements).

The library declares no permission and starts no location request, so apps that
don't show a location don't ask for one. An app that does declares
`ohos.permission.APPROXIMATELY_LOCATION` and `ohos.permission.LOCATION` in its
`module.json5`, with a `reason`, asks for them when the user wants to see their
location, and listens until the page goes away:

<Code code={region(examples, "location-updates")} lang="ts" />

## Heading

The arrow points where the device faces while the app passes a heading as
`userHeading`, in degrees clockwise from north, and shows the course again once
it's `undefined`. The compass needs no permission. An app reads it from
`@ohos.sensor`'s orientation sensor while the page shows, and turns the map with
it by following with `FollowBearing.HEADING`.

A rotation by the user keeps following but stops turning the map, and so does a
camera move by the app that sets the bearing, such as a tap on the
<Api of="CompassButton" display="compass button" />. The
controller's observable `followedBearing` becomes `undefined`, so a button can
turn the map with the heading again:

## Style the indicator

`userLocationIndicator` changes the color of the default dot and arrow, which
the accuracy circle takes too unless it has colors of its own. It puts images of
the style's own, or of the map's [content](/maplibre-arkui/content/), in place
of the default dot, arrow, and shadow, and puts the indicator under a layer of
the base style or the content with `below`. An image name of `''` draws none:

<Code code={region(examples, "location-indicator")} lang="ts" />

See <Api of="UserLocationIndicatorOptions" /> for every option.
