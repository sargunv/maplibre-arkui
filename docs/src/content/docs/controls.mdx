<Api of="MaplibreMap" /> draws ArkUI content over the map from its trailing
closure. Without a closure, it draws <Api of="DefaultControls" />: a scale bar
and a compass along the top edge, and the MapLibre logo and an attribution
button along the bottom edge.

## Choose a preset

Put a preset in the closure to choose the controls.
<Api of="FullControls" /> adds zoom buttons to the default controls, which helps
users with a mouse. <Api of="AttributionControls" /> keeps only the logo and the
attribution button.

<Code code={region(controls, "preset")} lang="ts" />

## Show no controls

An empty closure draws nothing:

<Code code={region(controls, "no-controls")} lang="ts" />

<Aside type="caution">
  Most tile providers require you to show their attribution. If you remove the
  attribution button, show the attribution somewhere else, as this example does
  with <Api of="AttributionText" />.
</Aside>

## Add your own controls

The closure lays out its children in a `Stack` that covers the map, so place the
controls the way you place any ArkUI component. Make containers transparent to
touches, so touches between the controls reach the map:

<Code code={region(controls, "custom-controls")} lang="ts" />

The controls need no controller. Besides those above, there are
<Api of="AttributionButton" />, <Api of="MaplibreLogo" />, and versions of the
compass and scale bar that appear only when they're useful:
<Api of="DisappearingCompassButton" /> and <Api of="DisappearingScaleBar" />.

## Pin content to a coordinate

<Api of="Marker" /> places its content at a coordinate and moves it with the
map. `alignment` chooses which point of the content sits on the coordinate:

<Code code={region(controls, "marker")} lang="ts" />

To draw many points, add them to the map's content as a layer instead; see
[Add data to the map](/maplibre-arkui/layers/).

## Point to a coordinate off the screen

<Api of="PointerPinButton" /> shows a pin near the edge of the map that points
toward a coordinate off the screen. Pad its parent to keep the pin away from the
map's edges:
