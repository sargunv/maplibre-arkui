The map shows a compass while it's rotated or tilted, and the style's
attribution. `scaleBarEnabled` and `zoomButtonsEnabled` add a scale bar and zoom
buttons, and `viewportInsets` keeps them all clear of app UI over the map, as
[the camera](/maplibre-arkui/camera/#keep-the-camera-clear-of-your-ui)
describes. The ornaments follow dark mode.

ArkUI content in the map's trailing closure, its `overlays`, lies over it, and
<Api of="MapMarker" /> pins content to a coordinate:

<Code code={region(examples, "marker")} lang="ts" />

Each ornament is also a component, <Api of="MapCompass" />,
<Api of="MapScaleBar" />, <Api of="MapAttribution" />, and
<Api of="MapZoomButtons" />, to place yourself:

<Code code={region(examples, "compass")} lang="ts" />

The sources' terms usually require their attribution, so show it, with
`MapAttribution` or the controller's `attributions`, when you turn
`attributionEnabled` off.
