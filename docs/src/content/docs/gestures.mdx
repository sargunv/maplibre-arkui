Drag, pinch, twist, and drag two fingers up or down to pan, zoom, rotate, and
pitch; double tap to zoom in, double tap and drag to zoom, and tap with two
fingers to zoom out. The mouse wheel, a touchpad pinch, and the arrow, plus, and
minus keys work too. A drag that starts on the map moves the map, even in a
`Scroll`. `gestures` turns some off, and `cooperative` leaves one-finger drags
and the plain wheel to a scrolling parent (see <Api of="MapGestureOptions" />):

<Code code={region(examples, "gestures")} lang="ts" />

Content over the map that handles its own gestures, such as a button, keeps
them.

## Clicks

A tap is a click, and a long press or a right click is a long click. The map's
`onMapClick` or `onMapLongClick` gets it first, and returns `true` to consume
it. Then the layers of the [content](/maplibre-arkui/content/) with features
under it get it, from the top layer down, until a layer's `onClick` or
`onLongClick` returns `true`. A click that nothing consumed goes to
`onUnhandledClick`:

<Code code={region(examples, "map-click")} lang="ts" />

A layer's handler gets the layer's features under the click, as FFI's query
returns them, and the click's point and coordinate (see <Api of="MapClickEvent" />). `hitPadding`
widens what counts as under the click, so small circles and thin lines are
easier to tap:

<Code code={region(examples, "layer-click")} lang="ts" />

While zoom is on, a tap waits 300 ms for a second tap, so a double tap zooms
without a click. Clicks reach the handlers whichever gestures `gestures` turns
off.
